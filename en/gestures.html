Hidden gestures & buttons

Some actions in Kadu are hidden behind a long press or a small button on a card. Here's the full list.

Home screen

ActionResult
Tap a category cardOpens the timeline with entries from that category.
Long press a category cardOpens category editing: name, icon, privacy, deletion.
The + button on a category cardCreates a new entry directly in that category.
Add category below the listCreates a new category. Without Premium you can have up to 6 categories.
The Week, Month, Year cardsOpen the timeline filtered to that period.

Entry card in the timeline

ActionResult
Tap the cardOpens the entry for viewing.
Tap a photo or videoOpens it full screen; swipe to see the others.
StarAdds the entry to favorites. Show them separately with the Favorites filter.
The ⋯ buttonMenu: View, Edit, Delete.

Media Library

Media library in the timeline gathers all photos and videos from your entries into one grid. You can filter it by type: Photos or Videos.

A long press on an item opens a menu:

  • Edit entry opens the entry the item belongs to;
  • Show in Timeline jumps to that entry in the timeline.

Book

Book in the timeline shows your entries as a journal. Swipe left and right to turn pages. Without Premium the first 20 pages are available.

Pop-up panels

Filters, the calendar, slideshow settings and other panels that slide up from the bottom close with a swipe down or a tap on the dimmed area above them.
