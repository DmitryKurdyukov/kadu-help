What is Kadu

Kadu turns photos, videos, voice notes and text into your personal story. It finds real memories in your gallery on its own — shots taken around the same time and place. Save them to your timeline, relive them as a slideshow with music, or flip through them like a book.

  • Everything on your deviceNo account, no cloud. Only you can see your entries.
  • PrivacyPersonal categories hide from the main timeline and lock with a PIN.
  • Only what mattersScreenshots and burst duplicates never make it into memories.
