Slideshow

The slideshow turns photos and videos from your entries into a film with music and smooth effects.

How to start

Open the Timeline tab and tap Slideshow.

Important: the slideshow is built from what's in the timeline now

If filters are on in the timeline — a category, tag, period or search — only those entries go into the slideshow. That's how you make a film about one trip or one year. Want to see everything? Reset the filters first.

If the filtered entries have no photos or videos, Kadu will tell you there's nothing to play.

Settings

While watching, open Slideshow settings. Changes apply immediately and the film starts over.

SettingWhat it does
SpeedHow long each photo stays on screen: Slow — 5 s, Normal — 3 s, Fast — 1.2 s, Very fast — 0.6 s.
Video soundVideos play with their original sound.
Speed up videosVideo pace follows the slideshow speed: a bit slower on slow, unchanged on normal, faster on the fast ones. A video always plays to the end.
Show datesShows the memory's date on screen.
Motion effectsSmooth zoom and pan on photos. Works at Slow and Normal speeds: at faster ones the photo changes before the effect has time to play.
Reverse orderShows the oldest memories first instead of the newest.
Background musicMusic plays during the show. Under Track, leave Auto to let Kadu pick a track, or choose one yourself: Winter, Calm or Energetic.

Free plan limit

Without a subscription you can watch slideshows for 3 minutes a day. When the time runs out, Kadu offers Premium. The limit resets the next day.

With Premium slideshows are unlimited.

Save as a video Premium

A slideshow can be turned into a regular video to share with loved ones.

  1. Set up the slideshow the way you want the video to look.
  2. Open Slideshow settings and tap Share as video.
  3. Wait until it's built (“Creating your video…”).
  4. Choose Save to Photos or Share.
Videos can be up to 15 minutes long

If the slideshow is longer, export won't start. Increase the speed or narrow the selection with filters — for example, pick one month.
