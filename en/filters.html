Filters & search

The timeline shows all entries from newest to oldest. Filters leave only what you need: certain days, one category, a tag or a word in the text.

Filter panel

Open the Timeline tab and tap Filters at the top. Choose your conditions and apply them.

FilterWhat it does
TextKeeps entries whose text contains these words. Letter case doesn't matter.
CategoryShows entries from one category only. All removes the limit.
FavoritesThe Favorites only switch keeps entries marked with a star.
OrderReverse order shows the oldest entries first.
TagsEntries with at least one of the selected tags. More in Tags.

Filters combine. If you pick the “Travel” category and the #sea tag, only sea entries from that category remain.

Calendar: a day or a period

Next to the filters at the top of the timeline there's a calendar button. While no dates are selected it reads All time.

  • Date: pick one day to see entries for that day only.
  • Period: mark the first and last day of a range.

Tap Apply to use the selection, or Reset to see all time again.

Shortcuts from the home screen

Some filters turn on right from the home screen:

  • The Week, Month, Year cards show how many entries you made this week (Monday to Sunday), month and year. Tapping a card opens the timeline already filtered to that period.
  • A category card opens the timeline with entries from that category only.
  • On This Day: the button appears if you have entries from this same day in previous years. It opens the timeline with that day's entries.

Slideshow, Book and Media Library depend on filters

This is the least obvious part. The Slideshow, Book and Media library buttons in the timeline use exactly what the timeline shows right now — including filters, search, dates and order.

Example

Want a slideshow of your summer vacation? Choose June to August in the calendar, select the #vacation tag and tap Slideshow. Only those entries make it into the film.

If the slideshow or book seems to show “not everything”, check whether a filter is still on.

FAQ

Entries disappeared from the timeline

Most likely one of these:

  • a filter is on or a period is selected in the calendar;
  • the entries are in a private category: the main timeline never shows them;
  • the app was opened with a fake PIN.
Why isn't my private category in the category filter?

Private categories don't appear in filters or the main timeline. Open such a category from the home screen by tapping its card. If a PIN is set, the app asks for it first.
