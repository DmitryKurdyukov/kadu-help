Tags

Tags connect entries by a common theme: a trip, a person, a project. Kadu has no separate field for them — tags are written right in the entry text.

How to add a tag

  1. Type # in the text followed immediately by a word, with no space: #vacation.
  2. Type a space after the word. Only then does it become a real tag.
  3. Check that the tag #vacation is highlighted in the accent color.
  4. Save the entry.
Finally made it to the sea #vacation #sea2025 and having coffee
The key rule: a tag must be highlighted

If a word with # stays plain text instead of turning colored, it's not a tag. It won't appear in filters and the entry won't be found by it. This usually happens when there's no space after the tag — for example, when it's the very last word of the text.

An entry can have as many tags as you like, anywhere in the text — at the beginning, middle or end. Just put a space after each one.

The tag isn't highlighted — what to do

Place the cursor right after the word and press space. If that doesn't help, delete the tag and type it again: #, the word, a space.

Tip

For tags made of several words, join them with an underscore or a hyphen: #birthday_party, #first-snow.

How to find entries by tag

  1. Open the Timeline tab.
  2. Tap Filters at the top of the screen.
  3. In the Tags block, select the tags you need. If there are many, start typing in the Search tags… field.
  4. Apply the filter.

The most used tags are listed first.

If several tags are selected

The timeline keeps entries that have at least one of the selected tags. For example, selecting #sea and #mountains shows both sea and mountain trips.

To deselect all tags, tap Clear next to the block title.

The tag filter also applies to the slideshow and the book. For example, select #vacation and watch a film about that vacation only. More in Filters & search.

How to change or remove a tag

Open the entry, edit or delete the word with # and save. The tag list updates automatically. A tag disappears from filters once no entry uses it.

FAQ

Filters say there are no tags yet

Tags appear only after you've used them in at least one saved entry. Make sure the tag is highlighted in color — plain text with # doesn't count as a tag.

Are tags from Day One or Apple Journal kept?

Yes. During import Kadu finds hashtags in the text of imported entries and adds them to the list.

A tag can't be found even though the entry exists

Check three things:

  • the tag in the entry is highlighted in the accent color. If not, add a space after it and save the entry again;
  • filters look for exactly the word after #: a space inside ends the tag;
  • the entry isn't in a private category: the main timeline doesn't show those.
