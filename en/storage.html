Where data is stored

No account, no cloud. All entries, photos, videos and voice notes are stored only on your device, inside Kadu.

What this means for you

  • No one but you sees your entries. We never receive your texts or photos — they aren't on any server.
  • No sign-up needed. The app works right away, even offline.
  • No sync between devices. Your journal lives on one phone; move it with export and import.

Photos are copied into Kadu

When you attach a photo or video to an entry, Kadu keeps its own copy of the file. So:

  • you can delete the shot from your gallery — it stays in Kadu;
  • photos and videos in Kadu take up separate space on your phone, on top of what your gallery uses.

There's also the reverse option: Settings → Others → Save photos & videos to gallery. When it's on, photos and videos taken with the camera right in Kadu also go to your phone's gallery.

How not to lose your journal

Deleting the app deletes all entries

Since data lives only inside Kadu, it's gone if you delete the app, reset or lose your phone. We can't restore it — we simply don't have it.

The only reliable safeguard is a backup. Make an export regularly and keep the file off your phone: in iCloud Drive, Google Drive, on a computer or in email.

Tip

Back up once a month, and always before switching phones, resetting settings or reinstalling Kadu.

Delete all data

Settings → Import / Export → Delete all data erases all entries, categories and media permanently. Make an export first if you might need the data.
